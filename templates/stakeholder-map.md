> 💡 *Fill this in during the first two weeks of a project, then review it at every milestone. Stakeholders change — a map you never update is worse than none, because it looks finished.*

## How to use this / So funktioniert es

1. **List everyone** who can affect the project or is affected by it — sponsors, users, managers, suppliers, regulators, even sceptics. When in doubt, add them; it is easier to remove a name later than to discover a blocker late.
2. **Score influence and interest** from 1 (low) to 5 (high). Influence = can they change scope, budget or timelines? Interest = how much does the outcome matter to them day to day?
3. **Place each person in the grid** below and pick an engagement approach for their quadrant. The quadrant decides how much of your time they get, not how important they are as a person.
4. **Agree the communication rhythm** for each stakeholder and write it down — vague promises ("keep them informed") are where misunderstandings start.

## The grid / Die Matrix

```
Interest ↑ 5 │ Keep informed        │ Manage closely
            │ (regular updates,     │ (involve early,
            │  invite feedback)      │  decide together)
            │                        │
          3 │ ───────────────────────┼──────────────────────
            │ Monitor              │ Keep satisfied
            │ (light-touch updates)│ (consult on decisions
            │                      │  that affect them)
          1 └────────────────────────┼────────────────────────→ Influence 5
            1                        3                        5
```

| Quadrant | Approach / Vorgehen | Typical rhythm / Typischer Rhythmus |
|---|---|---|
| Manage closely | Involve in decisions, co-create solutions, check in before milestones | Weekly or per milestone |
| Keep satisfied | Consult before decisions that affect them, share outcomes promptly | Every 2–3 weeks |
| Keep informed | Regular updates, demo invites, feedback channel | Weekly update or newsletter |
| Monitor | Light-touch updates only, revisit if their influence grows | Monthly or at milestones |

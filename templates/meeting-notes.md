Fill it in during the meeting, not after — decisions fade fast.

Rules for good action items:

- **One owner per action.** Shared ownership means no ownership.
- **A date, not "soon".** If the date is unknown, write "TBD" and add a
  follow-up date to agree it by.
- **A verb at the start.** "Send", "Review", "Decide", "Book" — not "Budget".

---

**How to use this template**

1. Copy it into your meeting doc and fill in the details section before
   the meeting starts.
2. During the meeting, capture decisions and actions live — assign an
   owner and a due date before moving on.
3. Send the notes to all attendees within 24 hours, and paste the action
   items into your tracker (see `status-report.md` in this repo).
4. At the next meeting, open with the previous action items: done,
   in progress, or blocked — no item silently carried over twice.
